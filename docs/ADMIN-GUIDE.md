# Website admin guide

For staff at Merciful Paradise Academy who update the school website.

Sign in at **`<website address>/admin`** with the email and password you were given. If you forget
your password, ask a super admin to set a new one (Users → key icon).

---

## The basics

- **Draft, Published, Archived.** Only *Published* items appear on the website. *Draft* is for work in
  progress. *Archived* hides an item but keeps it for your records — use it instead of deleting.
- **Changes are live as soon as you save.** There is no separate "publish the website" step.
- **Preview** (eye icon) shows an unpublished item exactly as visitors will see it.
- **Web address (slug)** is filled in from the title. Avoid changing it once an item is published —
  people may have saved or shared the old link.
- **Order** — the ↑ ↓ arrows in lists set the order used on the website.
- **Deleting permanently** asks you to type `delete`. It cannot be undone.

## Common tasks

### Post news or an announcement
1. **News → New article.**
2. Fill in the title, a short summary (shown on cards and in Google) and the article itself.
3. Choose a category, and add a featured image (right side).
4. Set **Status** to *Published* and press **Create article**.
   To publish later, choose a future **Publication date**: it appears automatically at that time.

### Add an event
**Events → New event.** Enter the date and time (Addis Ababa time). Tick *All-day event* for events
without times. Upcoming events appear on the homepage and the Events page automatically, and move to
"Past events" once they are over.

### Add photos
- **Gallery → New album**, save it, then drag photos onto the upload area. Use the ★ button to choose
  the cover photo and type captions under each photo.
- **Media library** holds every uploaded file. Click a file to add **alt text** — a short description
  for people who cannot see images (for example "Grade 8 students in the science lab"). Files missing
  alt text are marked "No alt".

Photos: JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB. Documents (PDF, Word, Excel) go in the *Documents* folder.

### Change the homepage
**Homepage** lists the sections from top to bottom.
- 👁 shows or hides a section; ↑ ↓ change the order; ✏️ edits its heading, text and button.
- **Hero slides** (further down) are the large photos at the top. Use wide, high-quality photos
  (at least 1600 px wide). More than one slide becomes a slideshow.
- The cards inside sections come from their own areas: Programs, News, Events, Gallery, and
  **Highlights** (Why MPA, Student life, Admission steps, Campuses).

### Edit a page
**Pages** lists every page. Built-in pages (About, Admissions, Contact…) keep their address; you can
edit their text and images. A note at the top of each page explains where the rest of its content
comes from. **New page** creates an extra page (for example "Uniform Policy") — to show it in the
menu, add it in **Navigation**.

### Update the menu
**Navigation → Main menu.** Add items, choose "Show inside dropdown" to put an item under another,
hide items with 👁, and reorder with ↑ ↓. Pages themselves are not affected when you remove a menu item.

### Reply to messages
**Contact messages** shows enquiries from the website's contact form. Open one, press
**Reply by email**, then **Mark as replied**. Archive old messages to keep the inbox tidy.

### Staff, FAQs, statistics
- **Staff:** published people appear on the Leadership & Staff page; tick *School leadership* for the
  top section. To show the page in the menu, enable "Leadership" in Navigation.
- **FAQs:** questions in the *Admissions* category also appear on the Admissions page.
- **Statistics:** the numbers under the homepage photo. Only use figures the school can confirm.

## Super admin only

- **Theme:** colours, card and button shapes, logos and the browser icon. The preview updates as you
  type; a warning appears if text would be hard to read. "MPA defaults" restores the original colours.
- **Site settings:** school name, motto, vision, mission, values, phone, email, address, office hours,
  map, **social media links**, the header button and the copyright line.
- **SEO:** how the site appears in Google and when shared; the health check lists things to fix.
- **Users:** add people (Admin or Super admin), set new passwords, deactivate or remove accounts.
  There must always be at least one active super admin.
- **Activity log:** a record of every change and who made it.

Everyone can change their own password in **My account**.

## Good practice

- Write alt text for every photo.
- Keep summaries short (one or two sentences).
- Check facts before publishing — the website is the school's official voice.
- Never share your password; ask a super admin to create an account for each person.
